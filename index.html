10Common questions

Questions,
answered.

What can Prixial build for us?+

Prixial designs and develops context-aware AI applications, intelligent retrieval systems, adaptive knowledge systems, and spatial or location-aware experiences.

Can Prixial work with our existing data and systems?+

Yes. We can connect an intelligence layer to existing knowledge sources, databases, APIs, and spatial infrastructure instead of rebuilding systems that already work.

Do you build production applications or only prototypes?+

We build production applications as well as the context, retrieval, relevance, and action layers behind them. Prototypes are used when they are the clearest way to test a system before production.

Which technologies and AI models do you use?+

We choose the most appropriate infrastructure for the system. The current stack includes Postgres, Supabase, pgvector, Google Maps Platform, Places API, and leading AI models.

Will we own the code and product?+

Yes. You own the product, code, designs, and decisions created for your system.

Can the system expand over time?+

Yes. We begin with the smallest differentiated layer and add deeper retrieval, knowledge, or spatial capabilities when user behavior proves they are needed.
